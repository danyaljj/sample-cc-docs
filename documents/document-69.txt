Steam valve FAQs – Jayant Valves
What is a steam valve?
A steam valve regulates, controls, and stops the flow of steam in pipelines, boilers, and industrial systems.
Are your steam valves IBR-certified?
Yes, Jayant Valves offers IBR-approved steam valves that are ideal for Indian boiler and pressure systems.
What types of steam valves do you offer?
We supply globe valves, gate valves, safety valves, pressure-reducing valves, and non-return valves.
What industries use your steam valves?
Our steam valves are utilized in power plants, refineries, textiles, food processing, chemical, and pharmaceutical manufacturing.
Do you supply steam valves outside India?
Yes, we are a recognized steam valve exporter from India, delivering globally with full documentation and standards compliance.
